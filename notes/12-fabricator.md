# 12 · v0.30 "Fabricator": compounds → gear with real properties

The plan came from [08-chemistry-loop.md](08-chemistry-loop.md) and the roadmap line "compounds → gear; stats from real
properties (closed loop)". v0.29 gave every recognised crystal real data (elastic moduli, band gap). v0.30 lets the
player turn stored crystals into gear whose numbers come from that data. The loop is now:
mine → refine → react → index → **fabricate** → mine deeper / refine faster.

## 1 · What the player sees
- A new toolbar screen **制造台** (after 精炼厂). It has a first-time tour (`fabricator`, 5 steps) and help entry "08  制造台".
- Left column: stored batches.
  - Crystals the index recognises come first, each with both possible stats (pick hardness, panel efficiency) and craft buttons.
  - Molecules and unindexed batches say why they can't be used.
  - With no crystal in storage, a hint gives real recipes: rock-salt Na→Al, Cl→N gives AlN; CsPbBr₃; a 2 Al + 3 O cell gives Al₂O₃.
- Middle column: pick heads (starter pick + crafted ones; equip / scrap) and a rock table.
  - The table lists eight rocks with their hardness and the hits needed with the current pick.
- Right column: refinery roof, 3 panel slots, the total speed bonus, and a link to the refinery.
  - The refinery level line shows "（含太阳能 +X%）".
- 规则与数据来源 overlay: the model, measured data and game simplifications, each labelled.

## 2 · Science (all sources in `data/fabricator.json`, generated by `dev/fabricator/build_rules.py`)
### Pick hardness
- Chen, Niu, Li & Li, *Intermetallics* 19, 1275–1281 (2011), doi:10.1016/j.intermet.2011.03.026:
  Hv = 2(k²G)^0.585 − 3, with k = G/B (Pugh ratio). G and B are the MP VRH moduli.
- The model is empirical and fails for very ductile solids.
  - Pure Cu, Fe and Pb give k < 0.4 and a nonsense ordering (Sn > Fe > Cu).
  - Their real hardness depends on processing (cold work, quench).
  - Rule: k < 0.4 → "不适用" (not applicable), never a made-up number.
- Pugh, *Phil. Mag.* 45, 823–843 (1954), doi:10.1080/14786440808520496: B/G > 1.75 is labelled 偏韧 (tough), otherwise 偏脆 (brittle).
- Sanity checks:
  - The Poisson ratio from B, G must lie in [0, 0.5] (e.g. MP's Na₂CO₃ G = 201 GPa is rejected).
  - Alkali compounds (Li, Na, Cs) and halides or hydrides (F, Cl, Br, H) are refused as tools (reactive or soft).
- Sample values:

  | Crystal | Hv (GPa) |
  |---|---|
  | Fe₃C | 7.9 |
  | SnO₂ | 9.4 |
  | Al₂O₃ | 14.3 |
  | AlN (P6₃mc) | 16.3 |
  | Al₄C₃ | 18.1 |

  α-Sn gets 4.1 GPa (valid, but weaker than the starter).

### Rock hardness
- Broz, Cook & Whitney, *Am. Mineral.* 91, 135–142 (2006), doi:10.2138/am.2006.1844, Table 3.
  - It gives measured Vickers microhardness of the Mohs reference minerals: talc 0.14, gypsum 0.61, calcite 1.49, fluorite 2.00, apatite 5.43, orthoclase 6.87, quartz 12.2, topaz 17.6, corundum 19.6 GPa.
  - Rock Mohs values (from `minerals.json`, ranges by midpoint) are converted by interpolating this table (log-linear between neighbouring reference minerals).
- Mixtures take their main minerals (mindat):
  - granite 6.5 (quartz + feldspar) → 9.15 GPa
  - basalt 6.0 (plagioclase + pyroxene) → 6.87 GPa
- Topsoil, sand and clay are loose and ignore hardness.

### Dig rule (game simplification)
- hits = ceil(base × min(3, rock_hv / pick_hv)) when the pick is softer than the rock, otherwise base.
- **Starter pick = 5.43 GPa** (apatite, Mohs 5, roughly a steel knife blade). This is a game value.
  - Limestone, malachite and hematite are unchanged. Granite, basalt, cassiterite, spodumene and pollucite are slower.
  - Nothing is blocked, so old saves never regress.

### Solar panels
- Shockley & Queisser, *J. Appl. Phys.* 32, 510–519 (1961), doi:10.1063/1.1736034.
  - The detailed-balance limit is computed numerically: 5778 K blackbody sun, no concentration, cell at 300 K.
  - Peak is 30.5 % at 1.25 eV. The table covers 0.2–4.0 eV in 0.05 eV steps.
- The gap is MP's GGA value. It usually underestimates the real gap, and the overlay says so.
- Metals and entries without a gap are refused.
- Effect: refinery speed × (1 + 2.0 × Σ efficiency) over at most 3 installed panels. The factor and slot count are game values.

## 3 · Iron and tin (so hematite and cassiterite are no longer dead ends)
- New elements **Fe** (铁, covalent radius 1.24 Å) and **Sn** (锡, 1.40 Å). The radius source is RSC, as for the other elements.
- Refinery processes:
  - `hematite_iron`: hematite + coal → 4 Fe, 35 s (blast furnace: Fe₂O₃ + 3 CO → 2 Fe + 3 CO₂).
  - `cassiterite_tin`: cassiterite + coal → 2 Sn, 30 s (SnO₂ + C → Sn + CO₂).
- The MP index was rebuilt for the 16 elements (`dev/chem_index/fetch_mp.py`, same filters as v0.29):
  - 1 835 crystals (682 experimentally observed), 949 formulas, about 1.3 MB.
  - 381 entries have elastic moduli. MP database 2026.04.13.
  - The PubChem molecule list is unchanged.
- The API key stayed in `.runtime/secrets/` and was not written to any file, log or commit.

## 4 · Code
- `scripts/fabricator.gd` holds the rules and all formulas (static), plus owned gear:
  - Gear state: `items`, `pick`, `panels`, `serial`.
  - Actions: `quote` / `craft` / `equip` / `toggle_panel` / `scrap`, with `serialize` / `restore` (strict validation).
- `LabState`:
  - Owns `fabricator`. `sync_gear()` pushes the pick hardness to `river_nature` and the panel gain to `refinery.power_gain` (it runs on tick, offline catch-up and load).
  - `product_snapshot` stores the index key on new batches (`index`, `index_match`).
  - `batch_index(batch)` resolves a batch. It uses the stored key, or identifies the batch from its work when the batch came from a known template.
- Limitation: batches harvested before v0.30 from a *substituted* template (e.g. Al/N in rock salt) don't record their template. Their identity is hashed into the id, so they stay unrecognised. Harvesting again fixes it.
- Save **v26**: adds `fabricator` (`{version, items, pick, panels, serial}`).
  - It loads every older save with the starter pick and no panels. v0.29 cannot read v26.
- `river_nature.dig` uses `Fabricator.dig_hits` and says "（X比岩石软，挖得慢）" when slowed.
